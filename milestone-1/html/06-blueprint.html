1. How this blueprint should be used

The PRD requires an Owner-readable prioritized finish list after the audit, using construction vocabulary. This is that list. It recommends sequence. It does not change scope. Each later milestone still needs Owner approval before work begins. Anything that is a rebuild, a payment system, a finalized subscription model, or a district not in the PRD remains a Change Order.

Governing recommendation

Finish the one live Room so it is the pattern, then copy the pattern into Construction and Trucking as new parallel Rooms. Do not melt Media & Technology into a generic engine first. Do not pour the Foundation under a half-wired frame. Do not open a district lot until it has real Veil, Board, Handshake, and Profile — or keep the Coming Soon label.

2. What “done” means for the whole engagement

PRD success: the City functions as the product it is meant to be — availability in and out, 24-hour default veil, percentage board, Handshake before reveal, verified-style profiles, community that brings people back, Construction and Trucking as real exchanges, Residential and Commercial flows, communities integrated, structural subscription hooks, then a hardened handoff. Today that sentence is true only for Media & Technology, locally, with honest empty lots beside it.

  1. Owner reviews this Milestone 1 pack (especially 00, 05, 06).
  2. Owner closes or defers PL-020–025 (names, anonymous profiles, monetization remaining parked, extra districts, hosted leftover project, Slack cadence).

4. When to pour the Foundation

Pouring (creating/linking a Supabase project, applying 0001–0020, setting env flags) is not a Milestone 2 prerequisite in this recommendation. It is a Change-control event under the existing “never connect without Owner approval” rule. Suggested earliest responsible moment: after 2.1–2.4 so screens actually hit the seam, after PL-004/005 have an Owner-accepted answer, and after a dedicated Doors Inspection against a throwaway project — not against production, and not using leftover CLI metadata as the project.

If the Owner wants Foundation first, that is their call. The honest cost: you will be debugging empty richContent, pending media, false isAdmin, and unwired screens at the same time.

5. Change Orders (do not sneak in)

  • Full rebuild or leaving React / TypeScript / the drawn Supabase Foundation.
  • Stripe or any in-platform commission/transaction handling.
  • Final prices, ads, or “the” subscription SKU.
  • Live SMS/email/push vendors.
  • New districts beyond Construction, Trucking, Residential, Commercial, Communities, shared Profiles, Matching Board.
  • Rewriting Media & Technology matching weights to be generic without Owner confirmation.
