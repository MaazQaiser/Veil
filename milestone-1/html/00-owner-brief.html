1. Why this pack exists

The PRD for THE CITY OF VAEL (August 2026) says Milestone 1 must be finished and reviewed with the Owner before any development milestone begins. This pack is that milestone. It is an inspection of the building that already stands — not a rebuild, and not permission to start pouring new Rooms.

VAEL is an availability network, not a job board. A person or business veils in when they are available, or veils out when they need someone available. Matching is by percentage fit. Full profiles stay hidden until both parties accept in a Handshake Room. That is the product this City is meant to be. The success question in the PRD is simple: does the City function as that product? This audit answers: parts of it do, in one district, on one device, in a prototype. The rest is labeled on the lot, drawn on the blueprint, or not built yet.

How to read this pack

Document 00 is for you. Documents 01–04 are the inspection of Framing, Foundation, Doors & Locks, and Utilities. Document 05 is the Punch List. Document 06 is the recommended finish order for Milestones 2–4. Nothing in this pack changes the product. Owner approval of Milestone 1 means “the map is accepted,” not “start building Construction tomorrow.”

3. The three truths to take from this inspection

Truth 1 — The City runs in the browser today

Every account, listing, Handshake, and message lives in that visitor’s own browser storage. Two phones do not share a City. There is no live Foundation connected to the running app. A full database blueprint exists as drawings (SQL migrations 0001–0020) and has never been the thing the product actually stands on at runtime.

Truth 2 — Only one district is a real Room

Media & Technology is Live. Contractor (the closest thing to Construction) and Trucking are Coming Soon placeholders: a landing page and a local interest form, zero listings, zero matching, zero Handshake. Residential and Commercial, as named in the PRD, are not districts in the registry at all. Real Estate is Early Access — also not a working Room.

Truth 3 — Continue, don’t rebuild, is the right call

The prototype is real. Veil in/out, 24-hour visibility, percentage matching, Handshake, profiles, documents, community, and city-wide navigation all exist and work for Media & Technology on a single device. The Framing is coherent. The Foundation drawings are serious. The job is to stabilize that, then add the PRD Rooms on the same pattern — not to start over.

6. What this inspection did and did not do

Did

Read the live application tree, the district registry, the Foundation drawings (migrations 0001–0020), auth and permission design, and the existing engineer notes in docs/audit/. Re-checked those notes against the current files. Recorded PRD-vs-repo disagreements as findings instead of smoothing them over.

Did not

Change product code. Connect a live database. Build Construction or Trucking. Prove row-security against a running server (none is attached to the app). Sign off Milestones 2–4. This copy of the workspace also had no git history and no successful dependency install during the audit window — Inspections cite the project’s own documented baseline rather than a fresh test run.

7. Open items that only the Owner can close

These are already listed in the PRD. They remain Owner decisions. Engineering should not invent an answer.

  1. Monetization. Advertising, premium visibility, homeowner-lead gating, pricing. Structural hooks only until you say otherwise.
  2. District order beyond the PRD list. The live registry also shows Nursing/Healthcare, Equipment, Government, Real Estate, Legal & Finance. Building any of those is a Change Order unless you add them to scope.
  3. Naming. The PRD says Construction Exchange. The City currently labels that lot Contractor. Residential/Commercial are not lots on the map. You should confirm the names before Milestone 2 frames those Rooms.
  4. Anonymous profile visibility. Prototype screens show profile details to visitors who are not signed in. The Foundation drawing would hide district profile details from those visitors. That is a product door, not a coding preference.
  5. Communications cadence. PRD proposal: Slack, updates every day or two, milestone demos. Still to confirm.

THE CITY OF VAEL is a real, working prototype of the product in the PRD — for one district, on one device, with honest labels on every unfinished lot. Milestone 1 is complete when you have reviewed this pack. Milestone 2 should not start until you approve that next scope in writing.
