Platform flow

The website, the Vael In dashboard, and the Vael Out dashboard.

The public site is shared. After sign-in, Vael In and Vael Out each open their own dashboard.

1. The public website

Anyone can look around without an account. The site opens on a night city. The headline is “The right people. When they’re available.” Search sends the visitor to Explore.

The top of the site stays the same on every public page: Explore Districts, Projects, Community, Sign in, and Go Visible.

2. Two dashboards

Go Visible asks what the person is here to do. That choice opens a matching account and a matching dashboard. Vael In and Vael Out stay on their own sides. Moving from one to the other is a separate sign-in.

Vael In dashboard

I’m available to work.

This person offers skills, services, and current availability. The dashboard is built around being visible and meeting people who are hiring.

Vael Out dashboard

I’m looking for someone.

This person needs someone available for a role. The dashboard is built around hiring and meeting people who fit that need.

Each dashboard greets its own side: “Hey Vael In” or “Hey Vael Out.” Visibility lasts 24 hours. When the window ends, the person can Vael again from that same dashboard.

3. How someone enters

Go Visible asks one question: What are you here to do?

01Vael In. I’m available for work. Setup builds what they can do, then they go visible.
02Vael Out. I’m looking for someone. Setup builds the hiring need, then they post.

Into the Vael In dashboard

  1. Create an account and choose Vael In.
  2. Set identity: handle, how they will use VAEL, and the district their matches come from.
  3. Build the profile people will match against: name, photo, location, headline, discipline, skills, and experience.
  4. Say what they can do, how they take part, and whether they are available now.
  5. Review, then go visible. They land on the Vael In dashboard.

Into the Vael Out dashboard

  1. Create an account and choose Vael Out. This setup asks who they need, on its own steps.
  2. What I need, who they are looking for, location, requirements, and timing.
  3. Optional details: tools, engagement, budget, links, documents, and a photo.
  4. Review, then post. They land on the Vael Out dashboard.

4. The Vael In dashboard

Home opens with Hey Vael In and the line “Your availability, profile, matches, and community.” Status reads Visible while the 24-hour window is open, with the hours left, or Not visible when it is closed. The action is Go Visible, until they are already visible.

The sidebar is Home, Districts, Matches, Handshakes, and Community. A profile card asks them to finish the profile so people can see what they offer.

01Home. Complete the profile and the current district. Then handshake requests, your matches, and community in the district.
02Districts. The worlds they are live in, and the profile for each.
03Matches. “People VAEL has identified as a strong fit for you.” These are people hiring who fit what this person offers.
04Handshakes. “People who want to connect with you right now.” A handshake starts when they accept a VAEL on the board.
05Community. “Take part with people in your district.” Posts read as availability and updates.

If nobody acts before the 24 hours end, the other person returns to the board. This person can Vael In again later if that person is still available.

5. The Vael Out dashboard

Home opens with Hey Vael Out and the line “Here’s what’s happening with people who match what you’re hiring.” Status reads Vaeled Out while the window is open, with the hours remaining, or Not visible when it is closed. Until they have posted, the action is Vael Out.

The sidebar is Home, Districts, Matches, Handshakes, and Community. A profile card asks them to finish the profile so they are matched with the right candidates faster.

01Home. Complete the hiring profile and the district. When both are done, home says “You’re all set.” Then handshake requests, matches, and community.
02Districts. Where the hire is based, and the profile for that district.
03Matches. “People VAEL has identified as a strong fit for what you’re hiring.” These are people who are available.
04Handshakes. “Professionals who want to connect with you right now.” A handshake starts when they request one from a match.
05Community. “From the Community.” Recent posts from across VAEL, written from the hiring side.

If a professional responds and this person does not act before the 24 hours end, that professional returns to the board. They can Vael Out again later if the professional is still available.

6. How the dashboards meet

01A visitor uses Go Visible and chooses Vael In or Vael Out.
02Vael In goes visible from the Vael In dashboard, with what they can do.
03Vael Out posts from the Vael Out dashboard, with who they need.
04Each dashboard shows the other side as matches, ranked by fit.
05A handshake is requested on one dashboard and accepted on the other. When both accept, the full profile and a private room open.
