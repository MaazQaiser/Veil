Client walkthrough

The website, Vael In, and Vael Out.

One website. Two ways in. Vael In is for someone available to work. Vael Out is for someone looking for a person.

The website

Start on the public site.

These links sit at the top of the home page, Districts, Projects, and Community. No account is needed to look around.

Vael In

I’m available for work.

This path is for the person offering skills and availability. Follow it from the website.

  1. 1
    Go Visible
    On the website, press the orange Go Visible button.
  2. 2
    Vael In
    Select “I’m available for work.”
  3. 3
    Next
    Create an account: name, email, phone, and password.
  4. 4
    Welcome
    Continue into profile setup.
  5. 5
    Your VAEL identity
    Handle, how you will use VAEL, and your district.
  6. 6
    Build your profile
    Name, photo, location, and what you do.
  7. 7
    What you can do
    The work you take, how you work, and whether you are available now.
  8. 8
    Review
    Edit any section, then go visible.

You land here

Hey Vael In

The dashboard shows your availability, your district, and people who are hiring. This account does not use the Vael Out hiring steps.

Vael Out

I’m looking for someone.

This path is for the person who needs someone. It uses its own steps after the account is created.

  1. 1
    Go Visible
    On the website, press the orange Go Visible button.
  2. 2
    Vael Out
    Select “I’m looking for someone.”
  3. 3
    Next
    Create an account: name, email, phone, and password.
  4. 4
    What I need
    Who is hiring, the role, and a short description.
  5. 5
    Who I’m looking for
    Discipline and skills.
  6. 6
    Location
    Where the work is based.
  7. 7
    Requirements
    Optional skills or certifications.
  8. 8
    Timing
    When you need someone.
  9. 9
    Additional details
    Optional. Then review and post.

You land here

Hey Vael Out

The dashboard shows people who match what you are hiring. The orange button is Create an Opportunity. This account does not use the Vael In availability steps.
